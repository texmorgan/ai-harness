# Cite a referenced source

Use this when adding or changing an artifact whose `origin` is `referenced`.

1. Confirm the artifact has a `source` field. If not, stop and ask for it.
2. Confirm a matching row exists in `SOURCES.md`. If not, add one with `Verified: No`.
3. Summarize in your own words. Do not reproduce passages.
4. Quote at most one phrase, under 15 words, and only when the exact wording matters.
